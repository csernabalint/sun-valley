# Hallmark Anti-Slop Design Runbook

This runbook covers the design gatekeeper that keeps Sun Valley Zrt.'s UI distinctive, authentic, and free from AI clichés.

---

## 1. What is "AI Slop"?

AI coding assistants naturally converge on generic frontend tropes:
* Centered hero sections with two pill buttons ("Get Started", "Learn More").
* Identical 3-column card grids with icons in colored circles.
* Neon blue/purple gradients floating over dark gray backgrounds.
* Generic Lorem Ipsum copy detached from real business realities.

Hallmark treats taste as an executable ruleset, enforcing mechanical quality gates before code is written.

---

## 2. Hallmark Operating Verbs

### `hallmark` (Default Build)
* Selects from 21 distinct macrostructures (Editorial, Asymmetric Split, Bento Grid, Industrial Technical Sheet, Horizon Splice).
* Applies the Sun Valley brand palette (`#5F2125`, `#E36527`, `#2D3628`, `#F5F2EE`).
* Audits against the 57 Anti-Slop Gates.

### `hallmark-redesign`
* Replaces page geometry and structural rhythm completely while preserving 100% of approved copy, brand assets, and value propositions.

### `hallmark-study`
* Studies external live websites or screenshots.
* Extracts typographic scales, spatial density, and micro-interactions into `docs/design.md`.
